About

Building at the edge of AI, apps and automation.

Farman Ahmad is a creative technologist who spends most days somewhere between an AI playground, an Android build, and a half-finished automation script. The work is less about a single specialty and more about a habit — picking up a new tool, taking it apart, and finding what it can build.

That curiosity shows up as mobile app concepts, prompt-engineered AI workflows, small automation systems that quietly remove repetitive work, and interactive interfaces built for the sake of trying an idea out. Design and code are treated as one practice, not two.

This portfolio itself is part of that lab — an evolving, hands-on record of what's currently being explored, rather than a fixed resume.
